About Us

Born from peace dialogue, for Acehnese civil society.

Aceh’s protracted conflict left deep wounds on civil society — lives lost, torture, displacement, loss of livelihoods, and eroded access to justice. Various people’s forums were attempted, but none had fully united the voice of civil society.

From this reality, 54 people from diverse components of Acehnese civil society attended the "Brotherly Dialog among Acehnese for a Just Peace in Aceh" in Washington DC on October 5–8, 2001, organized by the International Forum for Aceh (IFA) and the Global Peace Centre of American University. From this meeting, ACSTF (Acehnese Civil Society Task Force) was formed, supported by representatives of the Government of Indonesia and the Free Aceh Movement (GAM) who were also present at the dialogue.

Since then, ACSTF has continued to advance sustainable peace solutions to the Aceh conflict through dialogue and nonviolence — engaging Acehnese, Indonesian, and international civil society.

Mission & Objectives

Dialogue and nonviolence, for a just peace.

Three lines of work that guide every ACSTF initiative and partnership.

Mission

Advancing Peaceful Solutions

  • Seek and promote a sustainable peaceful resolution to the Aceh conflict through dialogue and nonviolence, with the involvement of the international community.
  • Facilitate the international community to strengthen the capacity of Acehnese civil society that upholds human rights and genuine democracy.
  • Reinforce the work already carried out by other civil society components in Aceh’s peace and democracy process.
Objectives

Consolidating the Civil Movement

  • Advocate for conflict resolution through dialogue and nonviolence, involving the international community.
  • Consolidate the civil society movement for peace and justice in Aceh.
  • Strengthen the bargaining position of Acehnese civil society through local efforts and international support.
Targets

Representation & Peace Structures

  • Support ACSTF as an institution that facilitates the consolidation of Acehnese civil society components.
  • Develop community sectors to achieve genuine representation of Acehnese civil society.
  • Build more coherent peace structures and elevate public discourse.
